<h2 id="for-in-순서">for-in 순서<a class="anchor after" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#for-in-순서"><span class="anchor-icon">#</span></a></h2>
<p>기존에는 브라우저마다 <code>for-in</code> 문의 반복 순서를 보장하지 못했습니다(브라우저마다 반복 순서가 다를 수 있었습니다). ES2020에서 <code>for-in</code> 순서를 보장되도록 스펙이 수정되었습니다.</p>
<h2 id="optional-chaining">Optional Chaining<a class="anchor after" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#optional-chaining"><span class="anchor-icon">#</span></a></h2>
<p>ES2020에 추가된 사양 중에 개인적으로 가장 유용하게 사용하는 기능입니다. 객체의 값을 안전하게 가져오기 위해서는 아래와 같이 작성되어야 합니다.</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes github-light-high-contrast github-dark-high-contrast" style="background-color:#ffffff;--shiki-dark-bg:#0a0c10;color:#0e1116;--shiki-dark:#f0f3f6" tabindex="0" data-language="js"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">var</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> street </span><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">=</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> user.address </span><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">&#x26;&#x26;</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> user.address.street;</span></span></code></pre>
<p>메서드를 호출 후 응답 결과가 <code>null</code>과 <code>undefined</code>일 경우 아래와 같은 예외 처리가 필요합니다,</p>
<pre class="shiki shiki-themes github-light-high-contrast github-dark-high-contrast" style="background-color:#ffffff;--shiki-dark-bg:#0a0c10;color:#0e1116;--shiki-dark:#f0f3f6" tabindex="0" data-language="js"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">var</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> fooInput </span><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">=</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> myForm.</span><span style="color:#622CBC;--shiki-dark:#DBB7FF">querySelector</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6">(</span><span style="color:#032563;--shiki-dark:#ADDCFF">'input[name=foo]'</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6">)</span></span>

<pre class="shiki shiki-themes github-light-high-contrast github-dark-high-contrast" style="background-color:#ffffff;--shiki-dark-bg:#0a0c10;color:#0e1116;--shiki-dark:#f0f3f6" tabindex="0" data-language="js"><code><span class="line"><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">var</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> street </span><span style="color:#A0111F;--shiki-dark:#FF9492">=</span><span style="color:#0E1116;--shiki-dark:#F0F3F6"> user.address?.street</span></span>

<h3 id="주의사항-2">주의사항<a class="anchor after" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#주의사항-2"><span class="anchor-icon">#</span></a></h3>
<p>Optional Chaining이 지원하지 않는 문법을 살펴보도록 하겠습니다.</p>
<ul>
<li>optional construction: <code>new a?.()</code></li>
<li>optional template literal: <code>a?.<code>string</code></code></li>
<li>Optional Chaining 후에 오는 constructor와 template literal: <code>new a?.b()</code>, <code>a?.b<code>string</code></code></li>
<li>optional property assignment: <code>a?.b = c</code></li>
<li>optional super: <code>super?.()</code>, <code>super?.foo</code></li>
<li>그 외의 예약어: <code>new?.target</code>, <code>import?.('foo')</code></li>
</ul>
